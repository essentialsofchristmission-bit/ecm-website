Jesus ends the Sermon on the Mount with a picture of two builders. Both heard His words. Both built houses. Both faced the same storm. The difference was the foundation.

The wise builder heard and obeyed. The foolish builder heard and ignored. Hearing sermons is good, but obedience is what makes our lives unshakable.

Today, pick one thing God has spoken to you about and put it into practice. Every act of obedience is another stone laid on the Rock.
