A single failure may feel random, but repeated failures often reveal a pattern. You may notice that you become more vulnerable when you are tired, lonely, stressed, offended, unoccupied, or seeking approval. Recognising the pattern allows you to respond before temptation becomes overwhelming.

Do not examine only what you did. Ask what happened before it. What captured your attention? Which voice influenced you? What desire became strong? What situation weakened your judgement? Patterns reveal the pathway that often leads towards compromise.

Awareness is not the same as condemnation. God reveals patterns so that you can walk wisely, not so that you will remain ashamed. Once you recognise the pathway, you can establish boundaries, seek accountability, and choose a different response earlier in the process.
