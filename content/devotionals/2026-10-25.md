proclamation: "I am not moved by what I see. My faith is being refined, and I will come out stronger in Jesus' name."
---
Trials have a way of revealing what we truly believe. When life is calm, it is easy to say that God is faithful. When the storm comes, our faith is tested and proven.

James does not tell us to enjoy the pain. He tells us to count it joy, because we know what God is producing in us. Patience, endurance and a deeper trust in the Lord are formed in the fire.

Whatever you are facing today, remember that God is with you in it. He is using this season to build something in you that comfort could never produce. Hold on to His Word. The same God who fulfilled His promises to Joseph, Job and Paul will fulfil His Word in your life.
