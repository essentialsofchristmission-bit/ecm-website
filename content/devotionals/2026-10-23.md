In Noah's day, people were eating, drinking, marrying and going about life as usual. None of these things are sinful in themselves. The problem was that they were so absorbed in ordinary life that they ignored God's warning.

Noah, on the other hand, listened to God and built the ark, even when it made no sense to those around him.

Jesus is coming again. Let us live each day with eternity in view, keeping our hearts right with God and sharing the message of salvation with those around us.
